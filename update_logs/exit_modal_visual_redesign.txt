# Update: Exit Modal UI Modernization

## Summary
The "Unsaved Changes" exit warning modal has been completely redesigned to look sleeker and more native to the operating system, replacing the previous oversized, cartoon-like design with a professional, desktop-standard aesthetic.

## Changes
- **Layout & Proportions (wrapper.html)**:
  - Reduced the modal width slightly from 400px to 380px.
  - Reduced the overall corner radius from a bulky 12px to a standard native 6px.
  - Decreased the oversized padding in the header bar and text area.
- **Typography & Alignment**:
  - Switched the text alignment from center to left-aligned, matching standard Windows/Mac dialog boxes.
  - Adjusted font sizes to feel more proportional and professional.
- **Buttons & Footer**:
  - Moved the buttons into a dedicated, lightly shaded footer area (#f8f9fa) with a subtle top border.
  - Aligned the buttons to the bottom-right corner rather than centering them.
  - Removed the heavy drop shadows. Buttons are cleanly styled with a classic pill-shaped 24px border-radius and crisp hover-state borders.
  - Changed the "Don't Save" and "Cancel" buttons to cleanly outlined secondary buttons to emphasize "Save" as the primary action.
