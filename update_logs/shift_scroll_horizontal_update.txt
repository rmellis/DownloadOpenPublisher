===============================================================================
OPENPUBLISHER UPDATE: FULL CANVAS PASTEBOARD & SHIFT + SCROLL HORIZONTAL SCROLLING
===============================================================================

DATE: September 12, 2026
MODULE MODIFIED: wrapper.html

PROBLEM SUMMARY:
1. When on the default 60% zoom, the page fits within the viewport width on wide
   screens. In previous builds, because the scaled page fit inside the viewport,
   horizontal scroll was prevented (maxScrollLeft was 0), meaning the user could
   not scroll the page left and right across the canvas like in the browser.
2. At 150% zoom, horizontal scroll previously stopped right at the page margin
   instead of scrolling past the page.

IMPLEMENTED SOLUTION:
Enhanced the webview injection in wrapper.html with a full pasteboard canvas engine:
1. Full Canvas Pasteboard Margins (60% Default & All Zoom Levels):
   - Establishes a full pasteboard margin (equal to the larger of 80% viewport width
     or the scaled paper width) on both the left and right sides of the paper.
   - Allows the user to scroll the ENTIRE page left and right across the canvas at
     any zoom level, including the default 60% zoom, 100% zoom, and 150% zoom.
   - Automatically centers the page on initial load and when clicking "Fit to Page"
     or "Fit to Width", while preserving full scrollability in both directions.
   - Smoothly preserves the user's relative horizontal scroll position during zoom
     slider and Ctrl+Wheel zooming transitions.
2. Complete Ruler & Margin Guide Synchronization:
   - Tracks the paper position dynamically with sub-pixel precision across all
     scroll offsets and zoom levels.
3. Universal Shift + Wheel Horizontal Scrolling:
   - Translates Shift + MouseWheel deltas into smooth horizontal scroll movements
     across the entire canvas.
